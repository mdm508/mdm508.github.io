This is a list of 326 videos from 小行星樂樂TV for people learning Taiwan Mandarin. YouTube [does not let viewers save videos marked “made for kids” to playlists](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9632097?hl=en), so I am keeping the links together here. Every video has subtitles that can be saved as SRT and used with LingQ, Migaku, or Chrome extensions that provide English translations. The list is ordered oldest to newest.
