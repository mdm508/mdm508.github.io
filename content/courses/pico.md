## Meet the Board

[**Raspberry Pi Pico WH**]({{< relref "/post/raspberry-pi-pico-wh.md" >}}) — What a microcontroller does, what you can build, the kit, an interactive pin guide, and editor resources.

## Project 1: The OLED Display

[**Project guide and roadmap**]({{< relref "/post/pico-oled-project.md" >}}) — Check the background knowledge, supplies, and wiring; see the full ten-phase plan.

1. [Meet the OLED]({{< relref "/post/meet-the-oled.md" >}}) — Connect the display, locate pixels, and draw your first text.
2. [Pictures as Data]({{< relref "/post/pictures-as-data.md" >}}) — Represent pictures with bits, bytes, binary, hex, and masks.
3. [Memory Organization]({{< relref "/post/pages-and-display-memory.md" >}}) — Practice a grid-to-list mapping, then find pixels in pages, bytes, and bits.
4. [The Frame Buffer]({{< relref "/post/the-frame-buffer.md" >}}) — Follow drawing into RAM, save a frame, and choose when to send it.
5. [Drawing Graphics]({{< relref "/post/drawing-graphics.md" >}}) — Fit shapes and character cells precisely, compose scenes, and animate frames.

Later phases will explore communication and the controller, then build a small driver.
